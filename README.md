# Chewy promo code: the codes that still work, and how to stack them without voiding your cashback

Most "Chewy promo code" pages are a wall of six-year-old codes that expire the second you paste them. The codes that do work at Chewy are mostly Chewy's own, and they're easy to find once you know the pattern: a new-customer gift card, a bigger gift card for basket sizes over $100, a small app discount, and Autoship — which isn't a code at all but is the largest single discount the company publishes.

There's a second layer most coupon round-ups skip entirely. Chewy cashback through a shopping platform pays a percentage back in actual dollars, and that cashback is voided if you apply a promo code the cashback platform doesn't recognise. So "which code is biggest" is the wrong question. The useful one is: on *my* basket, does the code pay more than the cashback I'd lose by using it?

That's what this covers — the codes, the Autoship maths, the membership, and the order of operations that keeps your cashback alive.

### WELCOME: the one for a first order

Chewy's new-customer offer is the highest-value single thing on this page if you've never ordered. Spend $49, enter WELCOME at checkout, and a gift card lands in your account after the order ships.

The detail that trips people up is that the $49 minimum is calculated **after** discounts. If you also switch items to Autoship, a $52 cart can drop below the threshold and the gift card silently stops applying. Build to roughly $70–75 before discounts if you want both. Chewy limits the offer to one per household and it deduplicates by address, email and payment method, so a second account on the same card is a good way to get a manual review instead of a gift card.

The gift card itself doesn't expire and stays on your account until you spend it.

### STOCKUP: bigger card, no new account needed

The $100-and-up code gives you a $30 eGift card and anyone can use it, not just first-time buyers. Prescriptions and vet diet products are excluded from the $100 minimum, which matters if a chunk of your basket is heartworm meds or a prescription diet — those dollars don't count.

Same timing as WELCOME: the card is issued after your order ships, sometimes within a day, sometimes a bit longer. Check your spam folder before emailing support.

### APP: $5 off, and it works for existing customers

The app code is the exception to the "new customers only" rule — you qualify if you've bought from Chewy on the web but never ordered through the app. $5 isn't exciting on its own, and it can't be combined with WELCOME on the same order. Treat it as a separate order discount, not part of a first-order stack.

### Autoship is where Chewy's real discounting lives

Autoship is free to enrol in and applies automatically when you set an item to repeat at checkout. The first delivery takes the headline discount; every delivery after that drops to 5% on eligible items.

The cap is what people miss. The first-order discount is capped at $20 in savings regardless of percentage, so a $300 first order does not get $105 off — it gets $20. A $60 first order is where the percentage actually behaves like a percentage.

You can pause, reschedule or cancel from your account with no fee, and the discount on the first order isn't clawed back. Chewy sends a reminder email a few days before each delivery processes, which is your window to skip or adjust without hassle.

Pharmacy Autoship is the bigger of the two at 50% off the first order, and it applies to prescription items only. Some deal trackers list a straight 20% off a first pharmacy order as well — that's the non-Autoship version. Both are separate from the retail Autoship discount, and neither one earns cashback (more on that below).

Here's the part worth reading twice. Chewy's terms for that cashback exclude "purchases made with vouchers or promo codes not featured on our platform." In plain English: apply a random coupon you found somewhere else, and you can lose the cashback on the *entire* order — not just on the discounted item.

So a $100 order with a $20 brand coupon isn't automatically the better move against cashback at the published rate. And a $100 order with STOCKUP — a $30 gift card — usually is, because $30 in future Chewy credit beats a few dollars in cash. The comparison you want is simple:

- Work out the code's value in dollar terms.
- Work out the cashback at the published rate on your order total.
- If the code is listed on the cashback page, you may get both — check before you assume.
- If the code isn't listed, you're choosing one or the other, not stacking.

Gift-card offers pay in Chewy credit you have to spend at Chewy, so they only count as savings if you were going to order again anyway. Cashback pays in withdrawable dollars, which is worth more per dollar to most people.

ShopBack's own instructions for Chewy are unusually specific, and skipping a step is the usual reason cashback doesn't show up. The sequence:

3. Empty your Chewy cart, close the Chewy app, then start your visit from ShopBack. You have to do this for every purchase, not just the first.
4. Use the browser extension or the app's in-app browser rather than navigating to Chewy directly.
5. Turn off adblockers and VPNs, and accept all cookies on Chewy's pages — Chewy can only confirm the transaction back to ShopBack if the cookies went through.
6. Decide on your promo code *before* checkout, based on whether it's listed on the cashback page.

## What never earns cashback on Chewy

- Pharmacy orders
- Prescription food and treats
- Gift card or voucher purchases, and gift card redemptions
- Orders using a promo code that isn't featured on the cashback page
- Fees, taxes, service charges and shipping
- Anything later returned, cancelled or refunded

If your basket is mostly prescription food and flea treatment, cashback isn't the lever — Autoship on the pharmacy side and the first-order discount are.

## Questions people ask before checkout

**Does Chewy have a student, teacher or military discount?**
No. There's no standing programme for any of those groups, so the savings have to come from the codes, Autoship and membership instead.

**Can I use two Chewy promo codes on one order?**
Deal-writers disagree on this. Business Insider reports that Chewy's cart lists applicable promotions in an "Available Savings" dropdown and that some can be combined, while other trackers state one code per order. The cart dropdown is the referee — whatever it lists as applicable is what you'll get.

**How much is the Autoship discount, really?**
35% off your first Autoship delivery, capped at $20 in savings, then 5% on every delivery after. Product pages occasionally show different rates on specific brands, so the item page wins over the headline.

**Is shipping free at Chewy?**
Orders over $49 ship free, and below that a $4.95 flat rate applies. Chewy's new-customer offer includes free shipping on the first order. The threshold has been changed before, so check the current figure on the deals page before building a basket around it.

**How long does the cashback take on a Chewy order?**
Tracked within roughly two days, confirmed after about 75 days, then withdrawable once your balance clears $5.

**Does ShopBack charge anything?**
No sign-up fee, no subscription cut, no standard withdrawal fee on the ordinary payout methods. The merchant's affiliate commission funds the whole thing, and your checkout price at Chewy is unchanged.

Pick the discount that pays the most in the currency you'll actually use, start your session in the right place, and keep the promo code away from the checkout until you've checked whether it costs you the cashback.
